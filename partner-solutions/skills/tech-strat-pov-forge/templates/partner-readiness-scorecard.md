# Partner Readiness Scorecard Output Template

Required output structure for the Partner Readiness Scorecard.
All 18 attributes must be present in the order below — never drop or reorder rows.
Source mapping notes (in brackets) show which POV section or research signal to draw from — remove them from final output.

**Status / Rank scale:**
- 🟢 **Strong · 3** — Clear evidence the attribute is met (named proof from POV or research).
- 🟡 **Emerging · 2** — Partial evidence; in progress or needs strengthening.
- 🔴 **Gap · 1** — No evidence, or a known deficiency that must be closed.
- *(blank)* — Not yet assessed; insufficient information from POV or research. Leave Status, Rank, Owner, and Notes blank.

**Population rule:** Fill Status/Rank, Owner, and Notes only where the POV or research provides evidence. Where there is no basis, leave the cell blank — do not guess and do not write "TBD" in the table body. Definitions are always filled.
