# Travel & Hospitality

**Salesforce Industry Cloud:** **Service Cloud, Sales Cloud, Marketing Cloud, Data Cloud, and Loyalty Management** (positioned as **Salesforce for Travel, Transportation & Hospitality**) — no dedicated "Travel Cloud" SKU.

**Market snapshot:**
- Global travel technology market: ~$11.8B in 2025, projected ~$28.6B by 2034 (CAGR ~10.3%) *(DataIntelo, 2025)*
- Hotel & hospitality management software market: projected to surpass ~$8.55B by 2035 *(SNS Insider, 2026)*
- ICT adoption in travel & hospitality growing at ~7.9% CAGR through 2030 *(GlobeNewswire / market research, 2025)*
- Legacy-system replacement (cloud-native, API-driven PMS and booking platforms) is the dominant spend driver *(industry reporting, 2025)*

**Key focus areas:**
1. **Unified guest/traveler profile & personalization** — single view across booking, PMS, loyalty, and on-property/in-trip behavior
2. **Direct booking & revenue management** — reduce OTA commission dependence, AI-driven dynamic pricing and upsell
3. **Loyalty & retention** — tiering, points, personalized offers, repeat-stay/repeat-trip growth
4. **Service operations & contact center** — reservations, guest requests, disruption and rebooking management

**Top aligned use cases:**
- 360° guest profile unifying reservation, stay/trip history, and preferences
- Loyalty program management with tiering and personalized offers
- AI-personalized pre-arrival/pre-trip upsell and ancillary revenue
- Contact center automation for reservations and service requests
- Travel disruption and rebooking management (airlines, cruise, OTA)

**Regulatory drivers:**
- PCI-DSS — payment card data security across booking and POS
- GDPR / CCPA — guest data rights, consent, cross-border transfer
- ADA / WCAG — accessibility of digital booking and self-service
- DOT consumer protection rules — airline refund, disclosure, and ancillary-fee transparency
- Local tourism / guest-registration and data-residency requirements (varies by country)

**Suggested KPIs** *(propose these in Stage 2 — confirm targets with partner)*:
- Direct booking share — target: ≥10 pt increase vs. OTA channels (offsets 15–25% OTA commissions)
- Guest satisfaction (NPS / review score) — target: ≥15% improvement within 2 quarters
- Loyalty enrollment rate — target: ≥30% of eligible guests/travelers
- RevPAR (or revenue-per-traveler) uplift from personalization — target: ≥8%
- Service request / reservation resolution time — target: ≥40% reduction

**MVP alignment signal:** Solution unifies guest/traveler data across at least one booking or PMS system and a loyalty or service channel, and the design partner is a named hotel group, airline, cruise line, or travel platform.
