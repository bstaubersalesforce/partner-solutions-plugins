# Manufacturing

**Salesforce Industry Cloud:** Manufacturing Cloud, Net Zero Cloud

**Market snapshot:**
- Smart manufacturing (Industry 4.0) market: ~$380B in 2026, projected ~$996B by 2032 (CAGR ~17.4%) *(MarketsandMarkets, 2026)*
- Global field service management market: ~$5.1B in 2025, projected ~$9.17B by 2030 (CAGR ~12.5%) *(MarketsandMarkets, 2025)*
- Unplanned downtime costs manufacturers up to ~$852M per week, with 61% of manufacturers hit in the past year *(Fluke Reliability survey, 2025)*
- Average direct cost of a manufacturing recall: ~$10M+ per incident *(Stericycle)* — unverified, pending refresh

**Key focus areas:**
1. **Connected asset management** — predictive maintenance, OEE tracking, asset lifecycle
2. **Dealer & channel management** — order management, partner portals, revenue forecasting
3. **Field service excellence** — dispatch optimization, technician enablement, parts logistics
4. **Sustainability & ESG reporting** — Scope 1/2/3 emissions, supply chain carbon tracking

**Top aligned use cases:**
- IoT-triggered predictive maintenance work order creation
- Dealer portal for order visibility and revenue tracking
- Field technician mobile workflow with parts inventory
- Warranty claim management and root cause tracking
- Scope 3 emissions data collection from suppliers

**Regulatory drivers:**
- ISO 9001 / ISO 14001 — quality and environmental management systems
- EPA / OSHA — environmental and safety reporting requirements
- EU CSRD / SEC climate disclosure rules — mandatory ESG reporting (2024+)
- Export Administration Regulations (EAR) — trade compliance
- FDA 21 CFR Part 11 — electronic records for regulated manufacturing

**Suggested KPIs** *(propose these in Stage 2 — confirm targets with partner)*:
- Unplanned downtime reduction — target: ≥20% within 6 months
- Mean Time to Repair (MTTR) — target: ≥25% reduction
- Planned vs. unplanned maintenance ratio — target: shift to ≥75% planned
- Dealer order fulfillment cycle time — target: ≥20% reduction
- Field service first-time fix rate — target: ≥85%

**MVP alignment signal:** Solution integrates with at least one ERP or IoT platform the customer already owns (SAP, Oracle, Siemens, Honeywell), and the design partner is an industrial manufacturer with a named asset management or channel challenge.
