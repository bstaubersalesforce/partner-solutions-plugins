# Energy & Utilities

**Salesforce Industry Cloud:** Energy & Utilities Cloud, Net Zero Cloud

**Market snapshot:**
- Global smart grid market: ~$73.85B in 2024, projected ~$161.15B by 2029 (CAGR ~16.9%) *(MarketsandMarkets, 2025)*
- DERMS (distributed energy resource management) software market: ~$1.42B in 2025, projected ~$3.29B by 2030 (CAGR ~18.3%) *(Mordor Intelligence, 2025)*
- Field service management software market: ~$5.10B in 2025, projected ~$9.17B by 2030 (CAGR ~12.5%) *(MarketsandMarkets, 2025)*
- Average cost of a major grid outage per customer: $3,000–$5,000 *(DOE, 2024 estimate)* — unverified, pending refresh

**Key focus areas:**
1. **Grid modernization & outage management** — DERMS integration, outage response, restoration tracking
2. **EV & clean energy customer programs** — EV charging management, demand response, solar/storage
3. **Field operations excellence** — crew dispatch, work order management, safety compliance
4. **Customer energy management** — usage analytics, rate optimization, sustainability programs

**Top aligned use cases:**
- Outage notification and restoration workflow management
- EV charging network monitoring and customer enrollment
- Demand response program enrollment and settlement
- Field crew dispatch with mobile work order execution
- Customer energy efficiency program management

**Regulatory drivers:**
- NERC CIP — critical infrastructure protection standards
- FERC Order 2222 — distributed energy resource market access
- State PUC mandates — EV infrastructure investment, clean energy standards
- IRA (Inflation Reduction Act) — clean energy tax credit administration
- OSHA / NFPA 70E — electrical safety standards for field work

**Suggested KPIs** *(propose these in Stage 2 — confirm targets with partner)*:
- SAIDI (System Average Interruption Duration Index) — target: ≥10% reduction annually
- Outage notification-to-field-dispatch time — target: ≥30% reduction
- EV charging program enrollment rate — target: ≥20% of eligible residential customers in year 1
- Demand response event participation rate — target: ≥25% of enrolled customers
- Field crew drive time reduction — target: ≥15% via dispatch optimization

**MVP alignment signal:** Solution integrates with a utility's existing OMS or ADMS, addresses a named reliability metric (SAIDI/SAIFI), and the design partner is a regulated investor-owned utility or large cooperative.
