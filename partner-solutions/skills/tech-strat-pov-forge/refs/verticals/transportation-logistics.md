# Transportation & Logistics

**Salesforce Industry Cloud:** **Service Cloud + Field Service** (fleet/asset ops), **Manufacturing Cloud** (account-based forecasting for shippers/carriers), **MuleSoft** (TMS/WMS/telematics integration), and **Data Cloud** — no dedicated "Logistics Cloud" SKU; grouped under Salesforce for Travel, Transportation & Hospitality.

**Market snapshot:**
- Global logistics software market: ~$16–19B in 2025, projected ~$39.7B by 2033 (CAGR ~9.4%) *(Precedence Research / Straits Research, 2025)*
- Transportation Management Software (TMS): ~$9.5B in 2024, projected ~$20.7B by 2033 (CAGR ~9.7%) *(Grand View Research, 2024)*
- Supply chain visibility software: ~$3.3B in 2025, growing at ~13.4% CAGR through 2035 — the fastest-growing sub-segment *(GMInsights, 2025)*
- Demand drivers: real-time visibility, route optimization, and cost-efficient transportation management *(market reporting, 2025)*

**Key focus areas:**
1. **Real-time shipment visibility & exception management** — proactive ETA, delay alerts, customer notifications
2. **Freight sales & quote-to-cash** — quoting, tendering, booking for 3PLs, brokers, and carriers
3. **Fleet & asset management / field service** — dispatch, maintenance work orders, driver enablement
4. **Customer service & self-service portals** — tracking, claims, document access, deflection

**Top aligned use cases:**
- Real-time shipment tracking with proactive exception alerts to customers
- Freight quoting, tendering, and booking workflow (3PL / brokerage)
- Driver and fleet dispatch with mobile maintenance work orders
- Customer self-service portal for tracking, claims, and documents
- Carrier onboarding and compliance/credential management

**Regulatory drivers:**
- FMCSA ELD mandate & Hours-of-Service rules — US commercial trucking
- DOT / PHMSA hazardous materials (hazmat) handling and documentation
- Customs & trade compliance — CBP filings, C-TPAT, Incoterms, denied-party screening
- IATA (air cargo) and IMO (ocean freight) operating and safety standards
- GDPR / CCPA — customer and driver personal data

**Suggested KPIs** *(propose these in Stage 2 — confirm targets with partner)*:
- On-time in-full (OTIF) delivery rate — target: ≥95%
- Shipment exception resolution time — target: ≥30% reduction
- Customer self-service tracking adoption — target: ≥50% of shipment inquiries deflected
- Freight cost per shipment — target: ≥10% reduction via optimization
- Quote-to-book cycle time (freight/3PL sales) — target: ≥25% reduction

**MVP alignment signal:** Solution integrates with at least one TMS, WMS, or telematics platform the customer already owns, and the design partner is a named carrier, 3PL, broker, or shipper with a defined visibility or service challenge.
