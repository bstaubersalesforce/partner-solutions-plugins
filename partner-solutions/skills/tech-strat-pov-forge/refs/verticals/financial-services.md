# Financial Services

**Salesforce Industry Cloud:** Financial Services Cloud

**Market snapshot:**
- Global banking technology spend: ~$650B annually, growing ~9% CAGR *(Gartner, 2024)* — unverified, pending refresh
- Wealth management software market: ~$6.28B in 2025, projected ~$18.77B by 2033 (CAGR ~14.7%) *(Grand View Research, 2025)*
- InsurTech software market: ~$23.54B in 2026, projected ~$132.71B by 2034 (CAGR ~24.1%) *(Fortune Business Insights, 2026)*
- Cost of financial crime compliance (KYC/AML), US & Canada financial institutions: ~$61B annually *(LexisNexis Risk Solutions, 2024)*

**Key focus areas:**
1. **Advisor-client relationship management** — holistic household view, goal-based planning, proactive outreach
2. **Lending & origination automation** — loan pipeline management, document collection, underwriting workflow
3. **Compliance & regulatory operations** — KYC/AML, audit trails, suitability documentation
4. **Insurance claims & policy servicing** — FNOL, claims routing, policyholder self-service

**Top aligned use cases:**
- Unified household financial profile for wealth advisors
- Loan origination and document management workflow
- KYC/AML case management and resolution tracking
- First Notice of Loss (FNOL) intake and triage
- Compliance documentation and audit readiness dashboards

**Regulatory drivers:**
- Dodd-Frank Act — systemic risk reporting, consumer protection
- FINRA / SEC rules — suitability documentation, communication records
- Basel III / IV — capital adequacy, risk reporting
- GDPR / CCPA — customer data rights and consent management
- BSA / AML — Bank Secrecy Act transaction monitoring

**Suggested KPIs** *(propose these in Stage 2 — confirm targets with partner)*:
- Loan origination cycle time — target: ≥35% reduction vs. baseline
- Advisor AUM per FTE — target: ≥15% increase within 12 months
- KYC/AML case completion within SLA — target: ≥95%
- Customer onboarding time — target: ≥30% reduction
- Compliance documentation audit-readiness rate — target: 100% at each review cycle

**MVP alignment signal:** Solution reduces a documented compliance cost or accelerates a regulated workflow with a named bank, insurer, or RIA as design partner.
