# Retail & Consumer Goods

**Salesforce Industry Cloud:** Commerce Cloud, Consumer Goods Cloud

**Market snapshot:**
- Global eCommerce market: ~$6.4T in 2025, ~20.5% of all retail sales *(EMARKETER, 2025)*
- Consumer goods trade promotion spend: ~$1T globally, with 50–60% ROI negative *(Nielsen)* — unverified, pending refresh
- Loyalty management market: ~$12.9B in 2025, projected ~$20.4B by 2030 (CAGR ~9.6%) *(MarketsandMarkets, 2025)*
- Cost of inventory distortion (out-of-stocks + overstocks): ~$1.73T globally in 2025, ~6.5% of global retail sales *(IHL Group, 2025)*

**Key focus areas:**
1. **Unified commerce** — omnichannel order management, inventory visibility, returns
2. **Trade promotion management** — planning, execution, and ROI measurement
3. **Consumer loyalty & personalization** — AI-driven recommendations, loyalty tiers, churn prevention
4. **Field sales & retail execution** — store visit management, planogram compliance, shelf intelligence

**Top aligned use cases:**
- Omnichannel order orchestration and real-time inventory
- Trade promotion planning and post-event analytics
- AI-personalized loyalty offers and churn scoring
- Direct-to-consumer subscription management
- Field rep store visit planning and execution tracking

**Regulatory drivers:**
- PCI-DSS — payment card data security
- CCPA / GDPR — consumer data rights and consent
- EU / US sustainability labeling mandates — product carbon footprint
- FDA FSMA — Food Safety Modernization Act for CPG food manufacturers
- FTC regulations — subscription billing and cancellation requirements

**Suggested KPIs** *(propose these in Stage 2 — confirm targets with partner)*:
- Trade promotion ROI-positive rate — target: ≥60% of programs
- Loyalty active member rate — target: ≥40% of eligible customer base
- Omnichannel order fulfillment cycle time — target: ≥20% reduction
- Field rep store compliance score — target: ≥90% on planogram/shelf standards
- Cart conversion rate improvement — target: ≥15% lift vs. control group

**MVP alignment signal:** Solution addresses a named trade promotion or loyalty inefficiency with a measurable ROI, and the design partner is a CPG manufacturer or omnichannel retailer.
