# Healthcare & Life Sciences

**Salesforce Industry Cloud:** Health Cloud, Life Sciences Cloud

**Market snapshot:**
- Global digital health market: ~$347.4B in 2025, projected ~$946.0B by 2030 (CAGR ~22.2%) *(Grand View Research, 2025)*
- US value-based care service market: ~$4.01T in 2024, projected ~$6.16T by 2030 (CAGR ~7.4%) *(Grand View Research, 2025)*
- Healthcare automation market: ~$44.75B in 2025, projected ~$69.06B by 2030 (CAGR ~9.1%) *(Mordor Intelligence, 2025)*
- Average cost of a hospital readmission: ~$15,000 *(AHRQ)* — unverified, pending refresh

**Key focus areas:**
1. **Care coordination & transitions** — post-discharge follow-up, care team communication, patient handoffs
2. **Patient engagement & access** — appointment scheduling, remote patient monitoring, health literacy
3. **Value-based care operations** — care gap closure, quality measure reporting, risk stratification
4. **Clinical & administrative automation** — prior authorization, referral management, denial reduction

**Top aligned use cases:**
- Post-discharge workflow automation (readmission reduction)
- Care gap identification and closure at scale
- Prior authorization routing and status tracking
- Population health cohort management
- Patient portal integration with care team workflows

**Regulatory drivers:**
- HIPAA / HITECH — data privacy and breach notification
- CMS Interoperability Rule (2021) — FHIR API mandates for payer/provider data exchange
- 21st Century Cures Act — information blocking prohibition, patient data access
- ONC / TEFCA — Trusted Exchange Framework and Common Agreement
- NCQA HEDIS measures — quality benchmarks for value-based contracts

**Suggested KPIs** *(propose these in Stage 2 — confirm targets with partner)*:
- Readmission rate reduction — target: ≥15% within 90 days of go-live
- Care gap closure rate — target: ≥25% of identified gaps closed within 30 days
- Prior authorization cycle time — target: ≥40% reduction vs. manual baseline
- Patient/member engagement rate — target: ≥30% of eligible population with active portal activity
- Time to care plan completion — target: ≥50% reduction post-discharge

**MVP alignment signal:** Solution addresses at least one named HEDIS measure or a CMS-designated quality priority, and the design partner is a health system, payer, or ACO.
