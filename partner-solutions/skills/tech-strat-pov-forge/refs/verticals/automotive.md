# Automotive

**Salesforce Industry Cloud:** **Automotive Cloud** (purpose-built — driver/household data model, vehicle-to-account relationships, connected-vehicle data). Commonly paired with **Data Cloud** (telematics/connected-vehicle data) and **Financial Services Cloud** (captive lending/leasing).

**Market snapshot:**
- Automotive cloud market: ~$33.4B in 2025, projected ~$118.5B by 2032 (CAGR ~19.8%) *(Coherent Market Insights, 2025)*
- Automotive software market: ~$36.9B in 2025, projected ~$111.2B by 2033 (CAGR ~14.8%) *(Straits Research, 2025)*
- Connected vehicle services is the fastest-growing layer, driven by OTA updates, telematics, and in-car commerce *(GMInsights, 2025)*
- Salesforce is a named major player in the automotive cloud market alongside AWS, Bosch, Continental, and BlackBerry *(Coherent Market Insights, 2025)*

**Key focus areas:**
1. **Connected vehicle data & in-car experience** — telematics ingestion, OTA, personalized in-car services
2. **Driver/owner lifecycle** — sales-to-service retention, household and vehicle relationship management
3. **Dealer network management** — lead distribution, inventory visibility, dealer performance
4. **Auto finance, leasing & captive lending** — origination, lease-end, and renewal management

**Top aligned use cases:**
- Unified driver/household profile across OEM, dealer, and connected vehicle
- Connected-vehicle telematics triggering proactive service outreach (predictive maintenance, recalls)
- Dealer lead management with real-time inventory visibility
- Service scheduling and post-warranty retention campaigns
- Auto finance origination and lease-end / renewal management

**Regulatory drivers:**
- NHTSA — vehicle safety and recall reporting (US)
- FTC Safeguards Rule — protection of dealer/customer financial data
- Connected-vehicle data privacy — CCPA, emerging US state connected-car privacy laws, EU GDPR
- UNECE WP.29 R155/R156 — cybersecurity and software-update management for connected vehicles
- Emissions / EV mandates — EPA, CARB, EU CO2 targets (shape product and go-to-market)

**Suggested KPIs** *(propose these in Stage 2 — confirm targets with partner)*:
- Dealer lead-to-sale conversion — target: ≥15% improvement
- Service retention rate (post-warranty) — target: ≥10 pt increase
- Connected-services subscription activation — target: ≥25% of eligible vehicles
- Recall / service campaign completion rate — target: ≥90%
- Service appointment self-scheduling adoption — target: ≥40%

**MVP alignment signal:** Solution leverages connected-vehicle or dealer data on Salesforce Automotive Cloud, and the design partner is a named OEM, captive finance arm, dealer group, or mobility provider.
