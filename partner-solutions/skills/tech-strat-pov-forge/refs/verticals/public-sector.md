# Public Sector

**Salesforce Industry Cloud:** Public Sector Solutions, Nonprofit Cloud, Education Cloud

**Market snapshot:**
- Global government IT spend: ~$600B annually, CAGR ~5% *(IDC, 2024)* — unverified, pending refresh
- US federal civilian IT spend: ~$68B in FY2026, with a record ~$75.7B requested for FY2027 *(OMB FY2027 budget request, via Federal News Network, 2026)*
- Government digital service transformation market: ~$20B in 2025, projected ~$50B by 2034 (CAGR ~10.5%) *(Verified Market Reports, 2026)*
- Average cost of paper-based benefits processing vs. digital: 3–6× more expensive *(Deloitte)* — unverified, pending refresh

**Key focus areas:**
1. **Citizen service digitization** — case management, permit and licensing, benefits delivery
2. **Grant management** — application intake, award management, compliance reporting
3. **Emergency & public safety** — incident management, resource coordination, situational awareness
4. **Program compliance & audit** — regulatory reporting, audit trails, fraud detection

**Top aligned use cases:**
- Benefits eligibility determination and case tracking
- Permitting and licensing workflow automation
- Grant application portal and award lifecycle management
- Emergency operations center coordination
- Social services case management with outcome tracking

**Regulatory drivers:**
- FedRAMP — Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program (mandatory for federal cloud)
- FISMA — Federal Information Security Management Act
- Section 508 / ADA — accessibility compliance
- NIST 800-53 — security controls framework
- State-level privacy legislation — varies by jurisdiction

**Suggested KPIs** *(propose these in Stage 2 — confirm targets with partner)*:
- Benefits application processing time — target: ≥40% reduction vs. paper baseline
- Case resolution within SLA — target: ≥85% of cases
- Permit/license approval cycle time — target: ≥30% reduction
- Citizen self-service resolution rate — target: ≥50% without agent escalation
- Grant compliance reporting on-time rate — target: 100%

**MVP alignment signal:** Solution is FedRAMP-ready or on the path to authorization, and the design partner is a named federal agency, state government, or municipal authority.
