# OG Tech Strategy Framework Reference

## Program Overview
The OG Tech Strategy Framework is Salesforce's structured GTM engagement for ISV partners building solutions on the Salesforce platform. Entry requires an active ISV Partner Opportunity and an approved GTP Tech Strategy Request. The framework's steps align to the Org62 Opportunity stages, and a completed POV is the artifact that advances the opportunity.

**Goal of the POV:** initiate a new contract (**03-Contract**) and provide the detail needed to scope the build (**04-Build**). A POV is "exit-ready" when the commercial model is locked (PADA signed) and the Solution Vision, Overview & Architecture section is complete.

## Org62 Opportunity Stage Alignment
The engagement progresses through the Org62 Opportunity stages. The POV is built across the early stages and is the gating artifact for 03-Contract.

| Org62 Opportunity Stage | What it means for the engagement | Engagement steps |
|-------------------------|----------------------------------|------------------|
| **00-Prospect** | Initial contact; is there a there there? | Step 1 |
| **01-Qualify** | Partner solution understood; SF fit established | Steps 2–4 |
| **02-Assess** | Full POV validation — gaps, value, market, stakeholders, proof points, readiness scorecard | Steps 5–13 |
| **03-Contract** | POV complete; commercial model locked; PADA signed — **primary POV goal** | POV exit criteria met |
| **04-Build** | MVP scoped and detailed; architectural runway addressed — **POV provides build detail** | MVP & Roadmap section |
| **05-In Market** | Solution live (e.g., AppExchange listed); GTM motion active | — |
| **06-First Report** | First proof points and outcomes reported against KPIs | — |
| **08-Dead** | Opportunity lost or abandoned | — |

## POV Section Definitions

### Problem Space
- **Problem Statement** — The specific pain the solution addresses, grounded in a named persona and context. Not a market observation.
- **Significance** — Why this matters at scale: market size, patient/customer impact, frequency of the problem.
- **Challenges** — Barriers to solving the problem today (organizational, technical, regulatory).
- **Risks** — What could prevent the solution from succeeding (partner risks, market timing, competitive threats).

### Vision
- **Vision Statement** — Single sentence: [solution] enables [specific persona] to [capability] so that [measurable outcome]. No vague transformation language.

### Solution
- **Solution Statement** — Plain-language description of what the solution does.
- **Benefits** — Top 3 measurable benefits to the customer.
- **KPIs** — 2–3 metrics customers use to evaluate success.
- **CSD** — Combined Solution Description (co-authored with PAM).
- **Product-Market Fit** — Four lenses: Viable (commercial potential), Feasible (buildable on Salesforce), Desirable (customer demand evidence), Ethical (privacy, safety, compliance).

### Use Cases
Three customer outcome statements using: persona + job-to-be-done + capability + measurable result.

### Target Markets
- **Primary** — Named market + named pilot/target customers
- **Secondary** — Named market + named pilot/target customers
- **Adjacent** — Potential expansion markets

### Personas
- **Admin User** — Internal configuration/management persona
- **Internal User** — Internal operational/frontline persona
- **External User** — Customer/patient/partner-facing persona

### Solution Alignment
- **Sales Play Alignment** — Which Salesforce Sales Play this solution supports (if applicable)
- **Blueprint Gaps** — Specific capability gaps on a Salesforce Industry Blueprint this solution fills
- **Identified Needs** — Stated needs from Product, Advisory, and Sales stakeholders

### MVP & Roadmap
- **MVP** — Minimum viable scope for initial release
- **Horizon 1** — Near-term roadmap items
- **Horizon 2** — Longer-term roadmap items
- **Architectural Runway** — Technical debt to retire before scaling

### ISV Commercial Model
- **App Type** — ISVforce (native managed package), OEM (embedded), Mixed/OSP, Reseller, Consumption
- **Licensing Model** — PUPM (per user per month), POPM (per org per month), Tiered

### Architecture Summary (high-level only)
- **Application Type** — Native, Composite, or describe hybrid
- **Integrations** — Key API frameworks and endpoints
- **User Interface** — Native LWC, Canvas, custom
- **Data Model** — Standard objects, custom objects, sync strategy
- **SF Cloud/SKU Dependencies** — Platform, Sales, Service, Industry Cloud, Add-On SKUs
- **External Dependencies** — Integrated systems, competing initiatives

## Readiness Scorecard Attributes
These are the 18 attributes scored in the Partner Readiness Scorecard output (Stage 12). Names match the scorecard exactly; the **Strong Signal** column is the cue for a 🟢 Strong · 3 rating.

| Attribute | Definition | Strong Signal |
|-----------|------------|---------------|
| Solution Tier | Priority determined by ISV Sales Lead, Tech Strategy Lead in consultation with other key constituents | Named tier classification |
| Executive Sponsor | Identify who is the 'champion' or sponsor for this partner | Named sponsor on both sides |
| Sales Fit (Sales Team Preference) | Customers willing to purchase the solution and there is real TAM for the solution | Named pilot customers, market sizing |
| Sales ACV Potential | The solution has the ability to generate pull-through and additional SKU sales | Quantified pull-through estimate |
| Commercial Model - ACV / PNR Potential | The solution has the potential to generate ISV/OEM ACV | Signed or near-signed PADA |
| Product Fit (Product Team Preference) | The solution specifically addresses a needed blueprint capability gap | Specific Blueprint gap named |
| Industry Fit (Industry Team Preference) | The solution provides tangible clinical/industry outcome improvement and aligns to core values | Named industry alignment |
| ISV Team Fit | The solution is packagable, transactable, has market fit and aligns to our ISV/OEM model | Managed package or packaging plan |
| ISV Tech Alignment | The solution aligns well to our platform and ISV-specific capabilities | Native/composite app, named SF deps |
| Executive Alignment Sponsorship | The solution has both partner and Salesforce executive alignment | Named execs on record |
| Industry Value Chain Alignment | The solution is aligned to industry-defined value chain capabilities | Named value chain capability |
| Salesforce Blueprint Alignment | The solution aligns to at least one capability on a Salesforce Industry Blueprint | Named Blueprint, named capability |
| Partner's Ability to Execute | The partner has existing partnership and/or alliance capabilities within their team | Named alliance team members |
| Partner's Ability to Sell | The partner's sales leadership is engaged in the partnership effort; existing partnership and/or channel sales capabilities | Named partner AEs, prior deals |
| Customer & Target Account Alignment | The partner shares the same or similar customers as does Salesforce | Named overlapping accounts |
| Partner's ISV Tooling Readiness | The partner has been trained and activated on Salesforce partner tools such as the PBO, COA and LMA | Active PBO/COA usage |
| Partner's Technical Engagement | The partner's lead technical team is engaged in the partnership effort | Named architects, active API work |
| Partner's Executive Engagement | The partner's executives are engaged in the partnership effort | Named exec, documented commitment |
