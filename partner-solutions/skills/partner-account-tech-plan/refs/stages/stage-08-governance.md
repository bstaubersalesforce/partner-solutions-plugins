# Stage 8 — Governance & measures

How the partnership is run and measured. The CAF *Govern/Secure/Manage* phase — but run as
**influence, not ownership.**

## Governance = INFLUENCE, not OWN
The **PAM / account team owns the Rhythm of Business** (the QBR/RoB cadence, the account plan
of record). PIE/PS is the **technical advisor**: ADRs, upgrade- and enforcement-readiness
reviews, the architecture re-review rhythm. Write the governance section as *how we advise and
influence the cadence the account team owns* — never as a cadence we claim to own. (This was a
flagged team weak-spot: strengthen by influence, not by taking the pen.)

## Define
- **Cadence** — the QBR/RoB rhythm at which the plan is re-reviewed (the living-plan cadence).
- **Decision rights** — who decides what (and where PIE/PS advises vs. the account team owns).
- **Joint success metrics** — the cross-cutting Measures (partnership health): # active exec
  relationships (≥3 before H1), # gaps closed per QBR, consumption growth, joint pipeline.

## Hand-off
These cross-cutting Measures sit at the top-level Measures line in the V2MOM
(`../v2mom-frame.md`); workstream-specific KPIs stay nested under their Methods.
