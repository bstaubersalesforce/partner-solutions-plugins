# Stage 7 — Roadmap & Go-Forward gap closure

The operational heart of the plan: a time-phased roadmap and the matrix that makes the plan
**living**. This stage runs *after* assessment (2), blockers (3), and the POV/red-team (4)
have returned their gaps.

## Time-phased roadmap
Lay out milestones per workstream across horizons: **H0** (now / next quarter), **H1**
(2–4 quarters), **H2** (18–24+ months). PoCs (Stage 5) and workshops (Stage 6) land at the
horizon where they prove or enable their workstream.

## Go-Forward Plan — Closing the Gaps (key element)
Build a single matrix that maps **every** gap — from the Current-State Assessment (Stage 2),
the Blocker Scan (Stage 3), and the Red Team (Stage 4) — to how it closes:

| Gap | Source | Closure action | Owner | Horizon | RAG |
|-----|--------|----------------|-------|---------|-----|
| … | assessment / blocker / red-team | the concrete action | named SF or partner owner | H0/H1/H2 | 🔴/🟡/🟢 |

Rules:
- Every Obstacle in the V2MOM must appear here with a closure action. No orphan gaps.
- Every row has a **named owner** (not a team) and a **horizon**.
- The **closure KPI** = # gaps moved RAG 🔴→🟡→🟢 **per QBR** — this is the plan's movement
  metric (the living-plan contract in `../best-practice-frames.md`). It is a cross-cutting
  Measure in the V2MOM (`../v2mom-frame.md`).

## Hand-off
The gap-closure KPI and the roadmap milestones feed Governance (Stage 8) and the V2MOM
assembly (Stage 9).
