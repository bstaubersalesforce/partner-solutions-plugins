# Stage 6 — Enablement / workshop (optional)

Where a workstream needs the partner's team enabled, plan a workshop/lab. Optional — include
only when adoption/enablement is a gap.

## How
Dispatch `workshop-forge` as a subagent with the relevant capability (e.g. Data 360,
Headless/HXL, Agentforce) and the partner's team profile from Stage 1. It produces a lab/kit
plan.

## Scope discipline
Tie each workshop to a Method and the adoption Measure it moves (CAF *Adopt* — see
`../best-practice-frames.md`). Skip the section if enablement is not a current gap.

## Hand-off
Workshops land in the roadmap (Stage 7) and feed the enablement/adoption Measures.
