# Stage 1 — Profile & trust posture

Build the partner picture an executive needs before any recommendation.

## Produce
- **Company profile** — what they do, segments, ownership (PE/strategic), ecosystem standing,
  how they build on Salesforce (ISVforce / OEM / platform), key products.
- **ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)** — who the partner sells to; the customer segments the
  partnership should target. Required in the overview.
- **Consumed technology** — what they consume from Salesforce today: OEM platform, licenses,
  SKUs, embedded capabilities. If a product catalog is accessible, summarize it; **if not,
  say so and move on** — do not block.
- **Pipeline / deals context** — current high-potential deals or recently closed large deals,
  sourced from the **PAM's forecast records** (we do not reconstruct pipeline from Org62).
  If unavailable, note it as a PAM follow-up.
- **Trust / relationship posture** — apply `../trust-posture.md`: the elevation-not-
  encroachment framing and the build-vs-buy / platform-trust tension specific to this partner.
- **Stakeholder / power map** — who holds budget, who holds trust, who blocks, who is dormant;
  current relationship threads and the ones to **activate** (target ≥3 active exec
  relationships before H1), with pairings.

## Draw on external sources
Generate the profile and stakeholder map first-hand from the external-source catalog in
`../external-sources.md` — **LinkedIn** for exec titles/tenure/recent moves (the power map +
key-person risk), **SEC 10-K/10-Q** for financials and the partner's own stated risks (public
companies only — private/PE-owned → use funding/ownership instead), **press/newsroom** for
traction and roadmap signals, **events** for the partnership-health read (cross-check Org62
event opps) and exec-activation targeting, and **analyst/review + funding/ownership** sources.
Honor the accuracy gate: confirm load-bearing facts from a primary source; AI-generated
canvases/snippets are leads, not facts.

## Degrade gracefully
Catalog and live pipeline are commonly unavailable — their absence is a flagged follow-up, not
a blocker. Primary-source (partner site) blocks: use the fallback chain (about/news pages,
LinkedIn, AppExchange, trade press) before declaring the profile thin.
