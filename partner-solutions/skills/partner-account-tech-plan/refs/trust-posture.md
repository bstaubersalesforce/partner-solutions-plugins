# Trust / relationship-posture overlay

For strategic ISV/OEM partners, the binding constraint is usually **trust, not technology.**
A tech plan that ignores the relationship posture will be architecturally sound and
politically dead. This overlay runs through the whole plan.

## Elevation, not encroachment

The governing value (carried into the V2MOM Values line). The partner must see the plan as
**making them the platform leader in their domain**, not as Salesforce advancing into their
whitespace. The nCino framing is the reusable template: *"<Partner> is the <domain> OS;
Salesforce is the platform underneath it."* Position every workstream as extending the
partner's leadership, grounding their AI/data, or distributing their reach — never as a
capability Salesforce will ship natively against them.

Watch for the roadmap-overlap trap (also a Red Team lens in `tech-strat-pov-forge`): if a
proposed value prop is something Salesforce could ship natively within ~18 months, name it and
reframe so it reinforces rather than threatens the partner.

## Stakeholder activation — break single-threading

A relationship single-threaded on one executive is the classic "engaged but reactive, same
issues year over year" failure mode. The plan must **name the current threads and the ones to
activate**, with pairings:
- Primary technical relationship (partner CTO/architect ↔ our strategist), with a manager-level
  exec tie-off.
- A trusted-pair (someone who already has the key exec's trust) to co-own.
- Named execs to *activate* on each additional workstream (e.g. the product owner for a
  product-innovation lane, the customer/success exec for a governance subscription).
- Target: **≥3 active executive relationships before H1.** This is a cross-cutting Measure.

A stakeholder/power map belongs in the profile (Stage 1): who holds budget, who holds trust,
who is a blocker, who is dormant.

## Cross-pollination lessons (reusable inputs)

Prior strategic-ISV engagements produced patterns worth carrying in — especially on the
tech-debt/packaging and platform-trust lanes:
- **Packaging modernization (Bullhorn):** profile each managed package before any 1GP→2GP
  move (API-version floors to convert vs. promote; Data-Cloud-metadata and large-file-count
  blockers); rationalize acquired products into a composable base+extension model; reframe
  Security Review as quality+docs, not cost; a per-org fleet placemat ("admin console of
  relevance").
- **Platform-trust elevation (nCino):** elevation posture, the 3 Moves (EXTEND / BUILD /
  DISTRIBUTE), Customer-Zero + joint CoE + co-sell, and positioning Data 360 as the grounding
  layer even when the partner's AI runs elsewhere.

Pull the specific lesson that matches the partner's situation; don't dump the whole list.
